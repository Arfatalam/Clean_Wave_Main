Download Monthly Statement

Select a month to generate its financial statement in PDF or Excel format.

Statement includes:
  • Total sales excluding cancelled orders
  • Total recorded expenses
  • Estimated profit (sales minus expenses)
  • Total monthly orders
  • Order counts by status
  • Monthly sales growth compared with the previous month
  • Individual order and expense details in Excel

These are management statements, not audited or tax financial statements. Estimated profit does not include costs that have not been entered into the Expenses section.
